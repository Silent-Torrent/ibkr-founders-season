## Reproduced problems and bounded fixes

The Cash Gang badge divided cash by any nonzero net liquidation value (NLV). A fictional report with cash −2,000 and NLV −10,000 therefore had a positive ratio and unlocked the badge; cash −1,000 and NLV 10,000 yielded negative progress. The badge now evaluates only finite cash and **positive** finite NLV. Negative cash remains not met with zero progress; zero/negative NLV is unavailable, never an unlock. Positive cash at 15% of positive NLV still unlocks; 14.99% does not. The three-language badge and unavailable explanations now state the positive-NLV condition. This is badge presentation, not a change to broker accounting or a claim that cash measures investment skill.

The CSV importer previously mapped known-section data rows to their latest header but silently ignored **populated cells beyond that header**. A fictional Trades option row ending in `A,Ex` was accepted with no errors, and the extra event code disappeared. Extra financial amounts could likewise vanish while the report appeared reconciled. The statement and Financial Instrument Information indexing paths now reject populated unmapped trailing cells with the existing localized `accounting_section_conflict` error. Empty trailing CSV cells remain acceptable. The real production-browser Worker test imports a fictional extra-cell report, observes the rejection, and confirms that the earlier synthetic Ledger/source/P&L remains in place.

The empty-record-type `Total P/L for Statement Period` row needs special care: it has no own header and borrows the current MTM header only when alignment is supported. An extra **nonempty** trailing cell is rejected. An extra **empty** trailing cell keeps the correctly aligned 750.243455 source. A synthetic Flex MTMP variant without the `Other` column retains a legacy-width headerless total row; its amount would map under `Code` if blindly forced against the shorter header. That row remains unparsed, with `statement_period_pnl` and `nav_bridge` marked `NOT_AVAILABLE` and report quality `warning`, while the supported Season P&L still imports. A separately constructed variant where this row **also** drops the blank `Other` position aligns with the shorter header and both reconciliations pass exactly. The old-width case is a known source-shape limitation, not a claim that the two reconciliations pass. A position-based rescue needs independent source evidence before implementation.
